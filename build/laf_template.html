413 Broome Street, the pink corner shop at Broome and Lafayette, on the ground floor of the Sing Tao Daily building. About 20 ft of Lafayette and the full 23 ft of Broome. Ceremonia's flagship since 2023. Eataly across Broome. Every building here is real, at its recorded height; the pink one is the store.

The building

413 Broome / 188 Lafayette

One building, two addresses. Sing Tao Newspapers bought the lot in 1997 and put up this five-storey office building in 1999; the paper's US office is upstairs. Before that the corner was a Cities Service gas station.

Two fronts, one corner

A corner shop with glass on both streets. Clay-plaster walls with carved niches, an onyx sink in a plaster island and a concrete floor, by Casa Angulo for Ceremonia, opened 30 June 2023.

Landmarks

The pink is a violation

The building is in a historic district, so paint, signs, awnings and storefront changes on the outside need a Landmarks permit. The pink paint on this store never got one, and neither did its sign.

IssuedOpen violation
May 2023Ceremonia sign and painting the building base, no permit

2016Murals on the Lafayette facade, and a wall sign, no permits

The owner's application to legalise a painted wall sign was withdrawn in October 2023. Eight violations are open on the building in all. Window graphics on the inside of the glass are the route that needs no permit.
